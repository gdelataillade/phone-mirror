- Security: paired-certificate/signature verification, HTTPS personalization,
  redirect/framing/allocation guards and four Rust dependency patch updates.
- Audio: playback stays on its audio loop; engine/configuration recovery, mute
  flushing and generation-safe buffering bounded to 200 ms of scheduled PCM.
- Automation: reject cancelled/stale responses; preserve main's concurrent
  read-only listing, input exclusion, observations/events and Camera stream expiry.
- Recovery: separate preparation/first-picture timing; fix independently reproduced
  historical-loss bookkeeping. Idle requires decoded progress through a recovered
  sync checkpoint. Existing deadlines and the 16-item queue remain.
- Diagnostics: allowlisted failure causes survive cleanup/reconnect without raw
  errors, device identity, media or input.
- Distribution: verify published assets before advertising the Sparkle feed.

## Lessons and known limitations

- Initial discovery trouble involved different Wi-Fi networks. Approved Finder
  Wi-Fi remained enabled, automatic sync off; effects were not isolated. No
  discovery fix was retained.
- An installed client overlapped earlier tests, invalidating affected Camera,
  stability and stale-video conclusions. Later global checks found only the
  candidate. Test one client at a time; root causes remain unproven.
- The latest run's initial USB session exited after about 54 seconds with `Audio transport closed`
  despite a reportedly connected cable; cable reliability/cause remain unknown.
  Another USB exit occurred during requested unplug.
- Wi-Fi handoff had one queue overflow/video-stall reconnect. A later orientation
  query failed about 67 seconds after reconnect while video arrived. Seven attempts
  included manual stops/reconnects, not seven spontaneous failures. USB was later
  reported despite the earlier unplug; physical transport state was unresolved.
- Static-screen silence can be legitimate. Orientation responses do not prove fresh
  pixels; an encoder-only freeze may still require manual reconnect.

## Window sizing and idle responsiveness — 5 November

The initial fitted-bar attempt left an oversized transparent window and remote
resize edges. Replaced its custom button hosting with original macOS controls;
fit/constrain the actual window to device geometry, updating only when geometry
or bezel visibility changes. Keep the bar fitted inside wider/full-screen space.
Native portrait/landscape/bezel-hidden checks confirm the window fits the phone
and repeated fitting is stable. Live minimize/restore and Finder/app switching
responded; no phone was connected.

Also skip video-state polling while no connection is active: a Combine reproduction
confirmed the old idle tick emits 100 redraw notifications in 100 calls, versus
zero with the guard. Active/reconnecting/closing sessions retain polling. The
local idle CPU samples changed from about 16% before the guard to 0.1% afterward
(0.00 CPU seconds over a three-second idle interval); this is a local observation,
not a general benchmark. Native zoom and switching to Finder/back also responded.
The reported switching delay was not conclusively attributed to one cause; an earlier
preview also stalled during native Window-menu automation.

Validation: 93 Swift tests and release build/signature checks passed. Temporary
checks were removed; the installed app and Wi-Fi behavior were not changed.
